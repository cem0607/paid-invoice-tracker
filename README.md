# Paid. — invoice tracker for freelancers that writes your reminder emails

**Live product page:** https://cem0607.github.io/paid-invoice-tracker/

Get paid on time — without the awkward "just following up…" email.

Paid. is a tiny **offline** invoice tracker for freelancers:
- tracks invoices (paid / due / overdue / 30+ days late)
- calculates late fees
- writes the follow-up email for the exact stage (3 / 10 / 20 / 45 days late)
- produces one clean PDF invoice you can print
- **one-time $19**, no subscription, unlimited clients, no account, works offline

## Free: the 5 reminder emails

## Honest limits
- It writes the emails — **you** copy them into your own email client. No auto-sending from your address.
- It does not move money. You keep your own bank / Stripe / PayPal.
- Late-fee rules differ by country — compliance is yours.
